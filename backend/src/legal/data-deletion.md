# Delete Your Account and Data

Bes gives you full control over your account and data. This page describes
how to delete it, exactly what gets removed, and how to request deletion if
you no longer have access to the app.

## Delete your account from the app (fastest way)

1. Open Bes and go to **Profile**.
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone** and tap **Delete Account**.
3. Confirm your password when prompted.
4. Confirm a second time — this step is irreversible.

Your account is deleted **immediately** once you confirm. There is no
waiting period, and we cannot recover it afterward — not even at your
request.

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your account and profile information (email, name, phone number).
- All trusted contacts you added.
- Your emergency event history, including captured location data.
- All audio/video recordings and their files in storage.
- All Covert Messages you sent or received, and your encryption key record.
- All active and refresh sessions (you're signed out everywhere).

Nothing is retained after deletion — this is a full removal, not a
deactivation or soft delete.

## Requesting deletion without app access

If you can't sign in (lost access to your device, forgot your password, or
uninstalled the app) and want your account and data deleted, email
**johnfarmer43@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your
account and request deletion. We'll verify the request and delete the
account manually within a reasonable time.

## Requesting deletion of specific data only

If you want only certain data removed (for example, a single recording or
Covert Message) without deleting your whole account, email
**johnfarmer43@gmail.com** describing what you'd like removed. Note that
some data — like emergency event records — may be something you'd prefer to
keep for your own reference, so we'll confirm with you before removing
anything you didn't explicitly ask for.

## Your rights

If you're located somewhere with statutory data-deletion rights (for
example, the GDPR's right to erasure, or the CCPA's right to delete), the
process above satisfies those rights: deletion is immediate, complete, and
free. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for more detail on what we
collect and why.
