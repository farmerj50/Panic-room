# Delete Your Account and Data

Bes gives you full control over your account and data. This page describes
how to delete it, exactly what gets removed, and how to request deletion if
you no longer have access to the app.

## Delete your account from the app (fastest way)

1. Open Bes and go to **Profile**, then tap **Settings** (top right).
2. Tap **Delete Account**. (You can also reach the same screen from
   Profile → **Danger Zone** → **Delete Account**.)
3. Review what will be deleted, then enter your password.
4. Confirm a second time — this step is irreversible.

**Save any evidence you need first.** Deleting your account permanently
removes your emergency recordings, location history, and stored evidence.
You can open and save recordings from Profile → **Evidence** beforehand.

**Bes Pro subscribers:** deleting your account does **not** cancel your
Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Bes shows a shortcut on
the Delete Account screen), or Google Play will keep billing you.

Your account is deleted **immediately** once you confirm. There is no
waiting period, and we cannot recover it afterward — not even at your
request.

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your account and profile information (email, name, phone number).
- All trusted contacts you added.
- Your emergency event history, including captured location data.
- All audio/video recordings and their files in storage.
- All Covert Messages you sent or received, and your encryption key record.
- Any connected TikTok or Instagram accounts, including the access tokens
  Bes stored for them.
- All active and refresh sessions — you're signed out everywhere, and any
  existing session stops working immediately.

**Connected social accounts:** when you delete your account, Bes also
revokes its access to your TikTok account automatically. Instagram doesn't
offer an equivalent for the kind of access Bes holds, so to fully remove
Bes from Instagram, also remove it in Facebook → Settings & privacy →
Settings → **Business integrations**.

This is a full removal, not a deactivation or soft delete, with two narrow
exceptions that exist to protect the people you added as trusted contacts:

- **Text-alert consent records.** If a contact accepted Bes emergency text
  alerts, we keep a minimal record of that consent (a keyed hash of their
  phone number, the time, and the version of the wording) for up to 4 years,
  so we can show they opted in. It is not linked to your account after
  deletion and is then deleted automatically.
- **Text opt-outs.** If a contact replied STOP, we keep a hashed record of
  their number so they are never texted again unless they reply START.

## Requesting deletion without app access

If you can't sign in (lost access to your device, forgot your password, or
uninstalled the app) and want your account and data deleted, email
**support@bes-app.com** from the email address associated with your
account and request deletion. We'll verify the request and delete the
account manually within a reasonable time.

## Requesting deletion of specific data only

If you want only certain data removed (for example, a single recording or
Covert Message) without deleting your whole account, email
**support@bes-app.com** describing what you'd like removed. Note that
some data — like emergency event records — may be something you'd prefer to
keep for your own reference, so we'll confirm with you before removing
anything you didn't explicitly ask for.

## Your rights

If you're located somewhere with statutory data-deletion rights (for
example, the GDPR's right to erasure, or the CCPA's right to delete), the
process above satisfies those rights: deletion is immediate, complete, and
free. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for more detail on what we
collect and why.
