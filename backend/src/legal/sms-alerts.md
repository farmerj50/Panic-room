# Bes Emergency Text Alerts

Bes is a personal safety app. When a Bes user activates an emergency, Bes can send a text message alert to the people they've chosen as trusted contacts, but **only to contacts who have opted in themselves**.

## Who receives messages

Only phone numbers whose owners have accepted an invitation. Bes users cannot sign anyone up: each trusted contact must give their own consent, and that consent applies only to the phone number it was given for.

## How opt-in works

1. A Bes user adds you as a trusted contact in the app and sends you a personal invitation link, usually by text from their own phone.
2. You open the link in any web browser. You don't need the Bes app. The page shows who invited you, the phone number that will receive alerts (partially masked), and the consent wording below.
3. You tap **Accept SMS Alerts** to opt in, or **Decline**. Each link works once and expires after 14 days.

We record your choice, the date and time, and the version of the wording you agreed to.

## Consent wording shown to contacts

> {{CONSENT_TEXT}}

## What messages look like

> Bes Safety Alert: [Name] activated an emergency. Location: [map link]. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

The location link is included only when a location is available.

## Message frequency

Messages are sent only when the Bes user activates an emergency. There are no marketing or recurring messages. Message frequency varies. **Msg & data rates may apply.**

## Opting out and help

- Reply **STOP** to any Bes alert to stop all messages. Reply **START** to resume.
- Reply **HELP** for help, or email **support@bes-app.com**.
- Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
